# DietSense Abstract Evidence Ledger

## Scope and method

This ledger checks factual statements in `docs/refactored/abstract-af.md`, `abstract-zh.md`, and `abstract-ieee.md` against the named source documents and official/public records. Repeated Chinese and English statements are grouped under one claim ID. Project-document support verifies that a statement is present as a proposal plan; it does not verify feasibility, accuracy, or eventual results. Proposed methods and future outcomes are therefore not represented as empirical findings.

The evidence statuses mean: **VERIFIED** = the source directly supports the bounded statement; **PARTIALLY VERIFIED** = only part of the wording or its status as a proposal is confirmed; **CONTRADICTED** = an authoritative source conflicts with it; **UNVERIFIED** = adequate supporting evidence was not found in the materials checked.

For internal project sources, the title is the filename or section named in the table; author and publication date are not stated in the source metadata reviewed, DOI is not applicable, and the workspace path is provided as the locator. Such records verify proposal content only.

## Unresolved evidence

1. Perform a reproducible related-work search before asserting a field-wide research gap or novelty.
2. Select and document an authoritative Taiwan food-composition source and local-recipe data method, including edition, nutrient definitions, access, and licensing/terms.
3. Define the annotation/reference-value procedure, dataset sampling, adjudication, reliability, and evaluation split before claiming dataset validity.
4. Justify the baseline, participant count, duration, outcome analysis, and candidate thresholds; complete consent/privacy/ethics review as applicable before recruitment.
5. Define and review safety and escalation rules with authoritative nutrition guidance and qualified expertise.
